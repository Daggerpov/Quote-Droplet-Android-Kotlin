```
Quote Droplet puts a fresh quote on your home screen. Pick a category, style the widget your way, and get one quote a day as a notification at the time you choose.

More than 5,000 people already use Quote Droplet on iPhone and Mac. Now it's on Android too.

HOME SCREEN WIDGET
• A new quote as often as you like, from every 8 hours to once a week
• Like a quote or skip to the next one right from the widget
• Resizes from a small square to a large card
• 8 color palettes, plus your own custom colors
• 9 fonts

DAILY NOTIFICATION
• One quote a day, at the time you pick
• A gentle water-drop sound

QUOTES FEED
• Scroll through quotes and save your favourites
• Copy or share any quote in a tap
• Search by quote or author, and tap an author to see all of their quotes
• Top: the quotes the community likes most
• Recent: every quote you've seen on your widget or in a notification

7 CATEGORIES
Wisdom, Motivation, Discipline, Philosophy, Inspiration, Upliftment and Love, or all of them at once. If you're into stoicism, you'll feel at home in Wisdom, with Marcus Aurelius, Seneca and Epictetus.

COMMUNITY
• Submit your own favourite quotes; approved ones are added for everyone
• Follow your submissions and their approval status

No account, no ads. Quote Droplet started as the quotes on my dorm room wall, and I hope a few of them find a place on yours.

Questions, ideas or a quote I should add? Use Send Feedback in the app.
```

## App content answers (as submitted 2026-11-18)

- Privacy policy: the TermsFeed URL above
- Ads: no ads
- Sign in details: no part of the app is restricted
- Advertising ID: not used
- Government app: no. Financial features: none. Health: not a health app.
- Target audience: 13-15, 16-17, 18 and over
- Content rating (IARC, "All Other App Types"): everything "No" except "features content that isn't
  part of the initial download" (quotes come from the server). Result: ESRB Everyone, PEGI 3,
  USK all ages, ClassInd all ages, 3+ elsewhere. User-submitted quotes were not counted as users
  interacting, because every submission is reviewed before anyone else sees it and the submitter
  isn't shown.
- Data safety: nothing shared with third parties, all data encrypted in transit, no accounts.
  Each type below is collected, not processed ephemerally, optional, for App functionality:
  - Personal info → Name: optional name/alias on quote submissions
  - Personal info → Email address: optional email on feedback, so replies are possible
  - App activity → App interactions: likes are counted on the server
  - App activity → In-app search history: search terms are sent to the server (and appear in its
    request logs)
  - App activity → Other user-generated content: submitted quotes and feedback messages
  - Device or other IDs: a random ID created on the device so "My Submissions" can list your quotes
  - The optional "can users request deletion" question was left unanswered.
